Why LSN

We don’t just train staff for a certificate. We prepare them for the job.

Care work is complex. Staff face safeguarding concerns, distressed behaviour, difficult decisions and situations where the way they respond can have a lasting impact. Our training connects knowledge with the situations practitioners actually encounter.

Real-world practice

Learning built around situations care professionals actually face.

Our courses connect theory with real practice. Learners explore realistic situations, professional decision-making and the challenges they may encounter when supporting children, young people and adults.

Safeguarding at the core

Safeguarding runs through everything we teach.

Safeguarding should never exist as an isolated subject. Our programmes encourage staff to recognise risk, remain professionally curious, understand their responsibilities and consider how everyday practice contributes to safer services.

Trauma-informed practice

Understand what may sit behind behaviour before simply reacting to it.

Our approach helps practitioners understand how trauma, previous experiences, emotional distress and unmet needs can influence behaviour. This supports more thoughtful, compassionate and effective professional responses.

Built for care services

Specialist learning designed around children’s and adult social care.

Our training is developed with the realities of regulated care environments in mind. The language, examples and learning are designed to feel relevant to the professionals completing them.

Why providers choose it

Training isn’t optional in care. Staff need to understand safeguarding, risk, behaviour, professional boundaries and their legal responsibilities — and inspection expectations are rising while workforce turnover stays a challenge. Poor training creates risk: weak staff knowledge affects safeguarding, behaviour management, recording, incident response and inspection outcomes. London Safeguarding Network gives providers training that is practical, sector-specific, relevant to the real challenges staff face — and evidenced, repeatable and linked to actual practice.

About the platform

What London Safeguarding Network is.

What it is

A specialist safeguarding and care training platform

London Safeguarding Network provides online and in-person training for children’s homes, adult care services, supported accommodation, foster carers, support workers, managers, schools, care agencies and regulated providers. It’s built for services that need clear, practical, credible training that can be evidenced during inspection, supervision, quality assurance and workforce development.

The platform combines safeguarding knowledge, therapeutic practice, care-sector realism, digital learning, downloadable resources, video teaching and provider-level training packages — not a dry course library, but training built around real risk, real practice and real inspection expectations.

Core message

Practical safeguarding and care training for real care settings

It helps providers train staff, evidence learning, improve practice and prepare for regulatory scrutiny. It is not just an online course library — it is a specialist safeguarding and care training platform built around real risk, real practice and real inspection expectations.
