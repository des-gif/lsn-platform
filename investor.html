Investment, partnership & licensing

How to get involved

London Safeguarding Network is open to investment, partnership and licensing. Investment could support course production, LMS development, accreditation, video filming, content editing, marketing, trainer recruitment and platform improvement.

Partnership opportunities include care providers, schools, fostering organisations, recruitment agencies, local authorities, training providers and safeguarding professionals. Licensing could allow providers or training companies to use LSN courses, resources and packages under licence — and the platform can grow as both a training provider and a safeguarding knowledge platform.
