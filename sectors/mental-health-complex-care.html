Training built for complex care

Trauma-informed, safety-focused, ready for escalation.

Working in mental health and complex care services means supporting people through distress, crisis and behaviour that can escalate quickly. Staff need to understand what may sit behind a person’s behaviour, how to co-regulate and de-escalate safely, and how to intervene when needed — while always safeguarding the person, themselves and their colleagues.

Our training brings trauma-informed practice together with the practical, safety-focused skills these settings demand.
